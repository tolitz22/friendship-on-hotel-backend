# LOG-8.4C — Hotel subscription lifecycle

Migrate before use. One current agreement per property and append-only revisions on mutations. All dates are Philippine calendar dates; expiry is exclusive (at midnight Asia/Manila). Trial is free, 1–90 days, only before first agreement. Snapshot price is the proposed recurring price, not a trial charge. Assign an active catalog plan to an unassigned/expired property or convert an unexpired trial immediately (unused trial days end). Monthly and annual periods use calendar addition without overflow; renew a paid agreement from its expiry if still current, or today if expired. Repeated month-end renewals follow the resulting day (e.g. Jan 31 → Feb 28 → Mar 28). Renewals preserve agreed price and limits even if catalog changes or becomes inactive. A new agreement after expiry uses selected current catalog terms. Revision checks on agreement and catalog reject stale edits; hotel lock serializes first assignment. No automatic renewals, collection, invoices, custom prices, scheduled plan changes, cancellation or suspension yet. Active denotes recorded commercial agreement, not proof of payment. Expiry is derived on read; no scheduler or access enforcement. Existing hotels remain unassigned until operator action. No hotel guest/folio/staff changes. No env vars. API limited to dedicated platform admins. No live customer mutations in development.
