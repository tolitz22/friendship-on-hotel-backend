# LOG-8.4F.2 — Manual SaaS payment verification

Migrate before deployment. Super Admin only. Record GCash, Maya, bank transfer or cash, positive integer centavos, transaction/receipt reference, external evidence locator and received date between issue date and Manila today. One payment reference per method across all invoices, uppercase/whitespace normalized, immutable even when rejected. Identical retry replays; conflicting reuse returns 409. No actual payment movement, file upload or provider validation. Use unique cash receipt references and external secure evidence storage; do not enter credentials/account numbers. Pending payments reserve remaining capacity but do not reduce outstanding; invoice locks serialize recording/review; only verified reduces balance. Rejection releases pending capacity; terminal records are immutable. Separate reviewer preferred; a sole operator may explicitly acknowledge self-review, recorded in the row. Review requires confirmation and note. Invoice list status is paid if settled, overdue if outstanding after due date, otherwise partially_paid or unpaid. Pending is reported separately. All filters use full matching balance, no page-only sums. No automatic renewal, void, refund, reversal, statutory receipt or revenue recognition. Rejected references cannot be reused until a controlled correction workflow exists. Existing hotel operational accounting untouched. No new environment variables. Do not use for actual customer charging until correction/renewal and acceptance are verified.
